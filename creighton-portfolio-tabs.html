About

Motivated by a love of serving others and hearing people's stories, I've naturally found my passion in sales. Built on three-plus years of customer service experience, I currently work at the Baylor University Engagement Center, where I call Baylor alumni to reconnect them with the university and help fund scholarships for current students.

I'm a second-year BBA student majoring in Professional Selling and Finance, and part of Baylor's competitive ProSales program — a hands-on environment focused on building trust, asking the right questions, and creating real value in every conversation.

This semester, that growth continues a world away. I'm studying abroad in Sydney, Australia, working through a managerial accounting course while learning to navigate a new city, a new culture, and new people — the same muscles I use every day on the phones, just stretched further. Adaptability, curiosity, and genuine connection translate everywhere.

At my core, I'm driven by faith and a desire to make a lasting impact — whether that's helping an alum reconnect with their alma mater, training a coworker, or simply listening to someone's story.
